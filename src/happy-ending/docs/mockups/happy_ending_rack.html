Wear, tape and screws as in every plugin of the suite (one seed per instance; click a piece of tape to write on it, drag a screw to turn it). Stepped knobs: drag vertically (Shift = fine), wheel = one step, double-click = default. M/S cycles ST → MID → SIDE on each band: the screen shows one stereo curve, or separate MID and SIDE curves as soon as a band works on M or S. On SIDE the high pass switches to a mono-bass scale (Off, 60–200 Hz). LISTEN solos one bell; while it is on, the other bands are locked. A band (or the stage) switched off locks its controls; only its IN button stays live. Bells narrow as their gain grows (Q-coupling). VIBE EQ: LOW does the boost-and-cut trick in one knob, TILT darkens or brightens around the mids, DYN pulls the LOW or AIR boost back on loud program; the amber ACT LED shows how much (dashed line on the screen = the static setting). GLUE: SSL-style bus compressor in parallel (its GR row is now in LEVEL), MIX 50 % by default; the screen scrolls the program level (grey), the compressor's gain reduction (amber) and the net reduction after MIX (dashed); WARM softens the attack. CLIP: soft clipper, level matched (quiet parts pass unchanged, peaks are rounded); the dot on the screen follows the program peaks against the dashed no-clip line, the amber CLIP LEDs show the dB taken off. LIMIT: look-ahead limiter on sample peaks; MODERN / PUNCH / DYNAMIC are styles of the same engine; SET forces a squash and locks everything but RELEASE until you press it again. FINAL: INFLATE (EFFECT, CURVE) or a safety CLIP pushed by up to +2 dB; only the controls of the selected mode are shown. METALHEAD: off by default; a brickwall limiter after FINAL, PUSH drives into the output CEILING. Top row as in every plugin: INPUT gain, LEVEL (IN/OUT peak meters, a clip LED per module in signal order, LUFS short-term and integrated — click to reset) and OUTPUT gain with the global BYPASS, which dims and locks every stage while the meters stay lit.
